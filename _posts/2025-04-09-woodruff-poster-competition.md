## Recap

Just wrapped up participating in our school’s poster competition. Only 40 graduate students were
selected to present their research, and I was very close to being a finalist. While I didn’t make it to
the final round this time, it was a valuable experience sharing my ideas and projects and learning about
the work of my peers. Hoping for another chance to participate next year!
